# Abell 1925: targeted third-field comparison

This is a targeted follow-up to the two previously inspected clusters, selected and bounded as recorded [before reading its images](../third-cluster-selection/PRESELECTION.md). [Botteon et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11720) identify PSZ2 G099.48+55.60 (Abell 1925) as a double-relic system and list z = 0.105. We streamed two exact named FITS members from the official archive, consuming **73,789,440 compressed bytes** under a 75,000,000-byte cap. The complete 82,105,034-byte archive was not downloaded or hashed. The extracted images total about 3.45 MB; their individual SHA-256 hashes, bytes consumed, source URL, server metadata and scanned member names are in the [manifest](../third-cluster-selection/manifest.json). No other members were extracted.

We use the existing catalog-seeded estimator with the same 135 kpc nominal Gaussian beam, 300/450/600 kpc radii, 0.1/0.2/0.3 mJy/target-beam cuts and independently ranked brightest 10% as the [two-cluster analysis](../two-cluster-physical-beam/README.md). At z = 0.105 and the stated flat cosmology, the target FWHM is 70.11 arcseconds. The original T50 and T50SUB beams differ, so each is smoothed to the target and T50 is interpolated onto the T50SUB grid. Gaussian kernel and finite common coverage are checked; this does not match full interferometric transfer functions. The published catalog coordinates and circular regions are not original author masks.

Signed T50 minus T50SUB pair-separation differences are **+116.05 to +116.72 kpc** at 300 kpc, **−35.43 to −34.50 kpc** at 450 kpc, and **−199.52 to −193.58 kpc** at 600 kpc, over the three thresholds. These are exploratory estimator outputs, not uncertainties, independent observations, published-position corrections or merger-age effects. The three fields were not randomly or blindly selected; the new field was chosen after studying the first two. Differences in redshift, source structure, noise and transfer function remain. The [three-field plot](three-cluster-comparison.png) uses the same descriptive grid and does not establish a population result.

## Reproduce

Run `python research/g099-paired-positions/analyze.py /path/g099-selected /path/rrsamp.tsv` with NumPy, SciPy, Astropy and Matplotlib. It verifies the two member hashes and catalog hash, target kernel covariance, aperture containment and common finite coverage. `compare.py` produces the three-field plot from the saved configuration tables. A separate Astropy calculation verified all nine separation differences from the 36 coordinate rows. The selected FITS, manifest, preselection and analysis are archived privately in Azure. The FITS grids return FK5 J2000 positions. Further work requires source-defined relic regions, noise covariance and a published simulation-clock baseline; no claim of physical bias follows.

A separate [computational replay](../g099-independent-reproduction/README.md) reproduces all nine configurations using a different convolution and interpolation implementation; it does not provide independent observational or scientific validation.
