# Published double-relic sample: descriptive distance ratios

This is a small transcription and descriptive check of the 11 selected observed mergers in Table 2 of [Jee, Park & Lee, arXiv:2605.00093v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00093). `distances.csv` contains only the reported redshift and central projected shock-to-shock and halo-to-halo separations and their stated symmetric uncertainties, in Mpc. The paper compiles individual measurements and their original sources; attribution belongs to those authors and the sources listed in its Table 2. This transcription has not been independently remeasured from maps and does not reproduce the full table, covariance or underlying instrument likelihoods.

`python analyze.py` computes halo/shock separation ratios and an illustrative first-order propagated standard deviation assuming independent errors. It does not calculate the authors' likelihood or their SIDM posterior. Ratios are positive at the reported central values; across these 11 selected systems, the median is 0.423 (range 0.179–0.634). Median reported fractional uncertainties are 14.5% for halo separations and 0.96% for shock separations. These summaries describe the paper's gold-selected sample, not a representative cluster population, causal trend, or new constraint. They do not compare individual observed systems to TNG catalog events.

The simple uncertainty formula assumes independent, small Gaussian errors; neither the full covariance nor the posterior shapes are provided by this table. For ZWCL1447.2+2619 the reported fractional halo-distance uncertainty is about 42%, so linear symmetric propagation is especially unreliable there. Shared systematics, covariance, sample selection and projection are not accounted for. The authors' analysis uses simulation-grid predictions, weak-lensing mass information and priors over merger phase, geometry, and other nuisance parameters, which this check does not have.

Input was transcribed from the linked Table 2, checked row by row against that source; two independent arithmetic checks cover ACT-CL J0102-4915 and ZWCL1447.2+2619. The script verifies positive values and unique names and records the input digest. Obtain the underlying posterior/likelihood, lensing and radio measurement provenance and selection function before a physical inference. No SIDM value, empirical novelty, or publication readiness is inferred.
