# MACS J1752: compact-model spatial comparison

The [official survey FITS package](https://lofar-surveys.org/clusters/PSZ2G071.21%2B28.86.html) includes `compact-model.fits` alongside T50kpc and T50kpcSUB. This exploratory check compares that nominal model to the [previous image-difference peak](../macs1752-difference-localization/README.md); it does not claim to reproduce the survey's subtraction pipeline or identify the astronomical source.

Using the FITS beam headers, the model is smoothed to the T50 Gaussian beam and scaled by the nominal beam-area ratio; celestial grids agree at three sampled positions. In a 30-arcsecond-radius region centered on the previously identified difference maximum, the pixel-wise spatial correlation is 0.998, and the two pixel maxima coincide to within the rounding of the stored peak position. The maps are derived from the **same observations**, the pixels are strongly correlated and the location was selected after inspecting the difference map: the correlation is descriptive, not a significance test or an independent confirmation.

The nominally beam-scaled model maximum is 2.52 mJy/target beam, compared with 26.55 mJy/beam for the difference peak (a ratio of about 10.5). Consequently the simple smoothed model is **not an amplitude reproduction** of the difference. The exact semantics, normalization and restoration of `compact-model.fits`, as well as the subtraction and imaging steps, remain unverified; a FITS BUNIT alone does not establish that this model can be convolved and interpreted as an ordinary restored image. A subsequent [normalization hypothesis test](../macs1752-model-normalization/README.md) shows that an unconvolved per-pixel component interpretation nearly matches this local amplitude; the actual model semantics remain unverified. The mismatch under the restored-image assumption is not a claim that the release is wrong. The close spatial correspondence is consistent with the patch being represented in the nominal model, but does not establish physical identity, membership, source type, or effects on a merger-clock inference.

Run `python research/macs1752-compact-model-check/analyze.py /path/to/selected-maps` with Astropy, NumPy, SciPy and matplotlib. Input hashes, FITS units, WCS agreement, positive-definite beam kernel and its discrete covariance are checked. The 30-arcsecond region and previously chosen peak are post-hoc; the plot uses a fixed common color stretch and may saturate the difference image. The extracted model and its parent archive are retained in private Azure.
