# MACS J1752: sensitivity to illustrative radio-image regions

This experiment uses the official compact-source-subtracted T50kpc radio map and published Botteon catalog coordinates documented in [the source-map record](../macs1752-processed-maps/README.md). It is a **region-choice diagnostic**, not an independent relic measurement, uncertainty interval, detection, merger age, or estimate of bias in the published catalog.

For each northern and southern published coordinate, use non-overlapping catalog-centered circles of 60, 90, and 120 arcseconds. Keep finite pixels brighter than 0.1, 0.2, or 0.3 mJy/beam, then calculate the intensity-weighted center of the brightest 10% of eligible pixels. These nine radius/cut combinations are illustrative choices, not the authors' masks, validated noise cuts, or a probability sample. The circles use the catalog centers, so agreement is not independent. Source-subtraction residuals, unrelated emission, excluded parts of a relic and correlated pixels can affect the result. The map beam FWHM is 14.73 × 10.64 arcseconds; the smaller offsets should not be interpreted as calibrated astrometric precision.

Across these choices, north offsets from the published coordinate are 0.18–4.67 arcsec; south offsets are 0.21–1.79 arcsec. Under an illustrative flat H0=70 km/s/Mpc, Omega_m=0.3 cosmology at z=0.366, the calculated pair separation is 2.6–7.9 kpc smaller than the separation of the catalog coordinates. This is a range over hand-picked masks on one image, not a confidence interval or an observed change in merger age. The masks may not capture all relic emission and do not reproduce the independent author extraction. Different beam and processing, sky-plane versus collision-axis or 3D coordinates, selection and a real noise model all matter before comparison with merger-clock models. See [Finner et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.02226), [Botteon et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11720), and [Lee et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21632) for their respective definitions.

Run `python research/macs1752-region-sensitivity/analyze.py /path/to/T50kpcSUB.fits /path/to/rrsamp.tsv`; NumPy, Astropy and matplotlib are required. Input hashes are checked against the adjoining processed-maps manifest. The CSV lists 18 individual relic results and pixel counts; an independent count verified nine unique configurations per relic and the top-10% selection counts. The WCS warning is documented in the summary. The source images have already been saved privately in Azure. No email was sent.
