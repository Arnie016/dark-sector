# PSZ2 G181: paired position sensitivity at a common beam

This is a second-field follow-up to the post-hoc [MACS J1752 paired-image diagnostic](../macs1752-subtraction-sensitivity/README.md), not a prespecified validation sample or population study. The already archived T50 and T50SUB maps of PSZ2 G181 have different original grids and restoring beams. Both were convolved to a circular 25-arcsecond Gaussian beam, converted using nominal beam-area ratios, and regridded to the T50SUB coordinates with bilinear interpolation. Common finite coverage is 100% inside the selected circles after the convolution-coverage test. This approximation does not reproduce the survey imaging pipeline or equalize its full transfer functions.

At catalog-seeded N and S coordinates, the same angular radii (60, 90 and 120 arcseconds), brightness thresholds (0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 mJy per target beam), and flux-weighted brightest-10% rule as the MACS test yield nine paired settings. North center offsets between the two images range from **0.19 to 5.46 arcseconds**, south offsets from **0.15 to 0.75 arcseconds**, and signed north–south pair-separation differences (T50 minus T50SUB) from **−0.88 to +0.58 arcseconds**. A separate coordinate-based recalculation checked all 36 saved rows. Individual offsets can project differently onto the pair axis, so a center displacement need not produce an equally large change in separation.

For context, the earlier MACS J1752 test found southern offsets of 0.01–15.90 arcseconds and signed pair changes of −15.80 to +0.20 arcseconds across its nine settings. The numerical contrasts are **not a controlled cross-cluster comparison**: the MACS maps retained their approximately 14.73 × 10.64 arcsecond beam, while this field uses 25 arcseconds; angular apertures correspond to different physical sizes, and beam matching, interpolation, field morphology, noise and catalog environments differ. The second field was selected after seeing the first field and comes from the same survey processing family. These are not uncertainty intervals, independent observations, a causal subtraction test, evidence of a general pattern, or a merger-clock measurement.

## Reproduce

Run `python research/psz2-paired-positions/analyze.py /path/to/extracted-maps /path/to/rrsamp.tsv`. The maps and catalog are archived privately in Azure under `research/psz2-model-normalization-20261107` and `research/psz2-multiwavelength-20261107/datasets`. The script checks input SHA-256 hashes, units, target-kernel covariance, common coverage and catalog identity; it writes all configurations to `variants.csv`, the summary and plot. Requires NumPy, SciPy, Astropy and Matplotlib. The algorithm uses catalog-centered circles, not the original authors' regions or a noise covariance. Cite [Botteon et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11720), [VizieR](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/A+A/660/A78), and the [LoTSS cluster release](https://lofar-surveys.org/planck_dr2.html). The [prior normalization test](../psz2-model-normalization/README.md) describes the matched-beam method and its unresolved model-unit caveat.
