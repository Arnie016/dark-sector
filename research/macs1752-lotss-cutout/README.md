# MACS J1752: LoTSS radio cutout and published relic coordinates

The [official LoTSS DR2 cutout service](https://lofar-surveys.org/cutout_api_details.html) returned a 570,240-byte low-resolution FITS image, with a 20-arcsecond beam and 143.65 MHz frequency in its header. [Della Chiesa et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02410) identify MACS J1752.0+4440 with PSZ2 G071.21+28.86. The [Botteon et al. VizieR table](https://vizier.cfa.harvard.edu/viz-bin/VizieR-3?-source=J%2FA%2BA%2F660%2FA78%2Frrsamp) gives two relic coordinates under that Planck name. Both fall within finite image coverage, separated by 6.361 arcminutes on the sky. The crosses mark published positions, not new image measurements. This mosaic includes compact sources and is not the higher-resolution, reprocessed multifrequency dataset used in Della Chiesa et al.; no shock, flux, spectral index, confidence interval or merger age is measured here.

The Botteon table reports a projected relic separation of 1941 ± 39 kpc, while [Finner et al., Table 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.02226) report 2323 kpc between centers of radio relic shock fronts. Their central values differ by 382 kpc, approximately 19.7% of the Botteon value. These products use different radio data and position definitions; cosmology, centroid definitions and other processing differences require a source-level audit before attributing the difference to any one cause. The numbers are not independent estimates of precisely the same quantity. The existing proposed merger-clock study specifically requires calibrated position definitions and uncertainties; no age shift or physical tension is inferred from this difference.

Reproduce with `python3 research/macs1752-lotss-cutout/analyze.py /path/to/cutout.fits /path/to/rrsamp.tsv`. Requires Astropy, NumPy and matplotlib. The manifest pins source URLs and SHA-256 hashes; the raw cutout and table are saved privately in Azure, not committed to GitHub. The program checks input hashes, image units and coordinate coverage. Survey cutout access and acknowledgements are described in the [DR2 release](https://lofar-surveys.org/dr2_release.html). No email was sent.
