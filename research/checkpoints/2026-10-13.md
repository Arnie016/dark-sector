# 13 October · finite sources do not remove every degeneracy

**State:** `HISTORICAL_REPORTED` · toy derivation; original integration absent.

For Gaussian sources near a locally straight fold, a pure caustic translation can be absorbed by a source/host offset. Two source sizes do not separate parameters entering only through one displacement.

Reported warm-dust/narrow-line changes −6.89% and −3.03% used invented widths/shifts and arbitrary normalization. They are not measured quasar ratios or a JWST sensitivity forecast. Numerical checks are reported, not rerun.

Curvature, morphology, astrometry, and arcs may add independent information beyond this limit. [RXJ1131 source-size context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23808v1) does not turn toy numbers into measurements.
