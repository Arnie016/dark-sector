# Sensitivity of merger selection to the elapsed-time threshold

This extends the [TNG-Cluster merger catalog](https://www.tng-project.org/data/cluster/) timing audit using the exact input CSV in `../timing-crosscheck/comparison.csv`. Both clocks share cosmology and simulation data; their difference is a field-convention sensitivity test, not independent validation or a confidence interval. Mass ratio is selected at >=0.1, and elapsed time must be nonnegative and no greater than the tested threshold. The ratio is defined at the secondary's peak mass, not necessarily at collision. Run `python analyze.py` with NumPy and Matplotlib; the input digest is recorded in `summary.json`.

At 0.5 Gyr, `T_coll` selects 61 rows and `TSC_full` selects 65, with four rows in only one set. At 1.0 Gyr, the counts are 107 and 103 with four in only one set. At 1.4 Gyr, both counts are 163 but the selected sets still differ by two rows: identical totals do not imply identical membership. See `sensitivity.csv` for the precomputed sweep in 0.05 Gyr steps and the figure for the curves.

These are selected catalog-event rows, not independent present-day clusters or observed merger rates. The catalog's documented temporal coverage is redshift dependent, especially at longer elapsed times; no completeness correction is applied. The cutoff sweep is exploratory and was not preregistered. It does not establish which clock is correct. The exact convention and snapshot ages, identity mapping, repeated events, selection and completeness remain to be resolved before inference or a paper claim. Refer to Lee et al. (2024), https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348194, and the simulation/data release references requested in the official documentation.
