# Independent Keck spectrum near the LoTSS optical counterpart

A 60-arcsecond cone search of the [Golovich et al. Keck/DEIMOS spectroscopic survey, table 7](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/ApJS/240/39) found 33 catalog entries. The closest to the optical position associated with `ILTJ175159.28+443849.6` in [LoTSS DR2 v1.2](../macs1752-photoz-v12/README.md) is `[GDW2019] J267.99546900+44.64944397`, at a separation of **0.0756 arcseconds**. The next closest catalog entry is 7.58 arcseconds away. The Keck catalog reports its spectroscopic redshift as **0.3708639 ± 0.0000217**, while LoTSS reports a photometric value of 0.3469112 ± 0.0308101 for its optical association. The difference is approximately 0.78 of the stated photometric uncertainty; this comparison does not establish a calibrated posterior or probability of membership. The reference cluster redshift is 0.366 ([Della Chiesa et al.](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202557936)).

The close optical positional agreement is strong evidence that the two catalogs refer to the same optical object, subject to astrometric and catalog-association checks. The Keck object is 9.19 arcseconds from the catalog radio position. The radio source is extended, and neither this positional match nor the spectrum proves that the optical galaxy is the origin of the radio emission, that it causes the image difference, or that it is a cluster member. A kinematic membership analysis would require a specified cluster velocity distribution and selection model. The survey is independent of the LoTSS redshift catalog; the association between radio and optical data remains the LoTSS catalog's association.

## Reproduce

`cone.tsv` preserves the complete response to the query recorded in its header: catalog `J/ApJS/240/39/table7`, center ICRS 267.9954559°, +44.6494251363°, radius 60 arcseconds, all fields, limit 1,000. The returned row count is below the limit. `python research/macs1752-keck-crossmatch/analyze.py` uses Astropy to compute spherical separations and writes `summary.json`, which records the raw response SHA-256. The LoTSS coordinates are from the [original v1.2 catalog row](../macs1752-photoz-v12/manifest.json); the previous reconstruction is needed to independently recheck them. Cite [Golovich et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.01347) and the VizieR catalog DOI 10.26093/cds/vizier.22400039. This is a targeted query, not an exhaustive search of every spectroscopic survey.

A follow-up [velocity-context comparison](../macs1752-keck-velocity-context/README.md) places this optical spectrum relative to the published two-subcluster velocity scales; it is descriptive and does not assign membership.
