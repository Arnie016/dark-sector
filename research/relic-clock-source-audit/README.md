# Radio-relic clock: source and data audit

**Decision: the baseline cannot yet be reproduced.** This audit reconnects existing local and Azure assets to the exact source products needed for the leading merger-clock study. It is not a new measurement or a completed manuscript.

## What the original study actually used

[Lee et al. (2026), sections II.1–II.2 and IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21632) combined TNG-Cluster and TNG300 across eight full snapshots between redshift 0 and 1. They identified shock-cell radio structures in 3D, selected and visually checked relic pairs, and associated them with merger events under a specified rule: the most massive subcluster passing pericenter between consecutive full snapshots, retaining the previous association if the relic pair already existed. The paper reports 578 double-relic **systems** after selection, not 578 independently verified host clusters. Its clock uses 3D emissivity-weighted centers; the published regression covers normalized separations 1–3.5. A follow-up requires both selected and excluded systems, stable identity across times, and per-event association choices to audit selection and avoid train/test leakage.

## Available assets and access

- The official [radio gallery](https://www.tng-project.org/cluster/gallery/lee23/) describes 1.4 GHz rendered views of 352 z=0 targets, three directions each. The earlier [Lee et al. (2024) paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.06340) says it released images and data for approximately 300 radio relics, and the final published paper points to the [cluster gallery](https://www.tng-project.org/cluster/gallery/). We found and previously archived three PNGs for snapshot 99 subhalo 0, with matching [gas and X-ray illustrations](../multiwavelength-gallery-subhalo0/README.md). The direct [radio provenance and manifest](../relic-gallery-subhalo0/README.md) are already in this repository. These plots provide qualitative context; they do not establish access to calibrated numerical maps, the 2026 selected sample, matched shock cells, or events at other snapshots.
- The corrected merger catalog contains event histories. A previous [main-history check](../event-main-trajectory-check/README.md) finds nine rows consistent with the saved snapshot-99 subhalo-0 position and velocity. That is not a validated merger tree or verified mapping between any rendered radio feature and a specific event.
- The 2026 paper points to the general public TNG data service but does not, in the sections inspected, provide a direct downloadable event-by-event relic selection table or analysis code. This is a bounded search result, not proof that such products do not exist. The 2024 paper says additional publication data are available on request. Public plots and the 2024 collection should not be assumed identical to the 2026 selected sample.

## Minimum data package for baseline reproduction

1. A versioned row-level list of **all candidates**, including single/non-detected/excluded cases, simulation name, snapshot, unique host and progenitor/event identifiers, and crosswalk to the corrected merger catalog. Include association changes and visual-inspection decisions, rather than selected objects alone.
2. For each candidate: the two 3D emissivity-weighted centers and reference center, coordinates and units/frame; relic luminosities, extents, detection and classification flags, the exact R500c definition and epoch, first-pericenter time and collision time definition. Include enough provenance to distinguish simulation snapshots and multiple events in one host.
3. The original sample-selection/association code or equivalent documented algorithms and configuration, including grouping, AGN cell removal, exclusion thresholds, and fitting procedure; fit uncertainties and residuals or underlying row-level inputs. Preserve 3D-versus-projected distinctions and field definitions.
4. For the proposed sensitivity test beyond baseline reproduction: calibrated radio-emissivity maps or shock-cell data with projection geometry, selection masks, dimensions and physical coordinates, plus instrument beam/noise models where survey-like perturbations are tested. A rendered PNG alone cannot substitute for these inputs.
5. A verified host/tree crosswalk to isolate independent clusters and related zooms before splitting development and held-out sets. Hash and record each supplied file, software version, access conditions, sample exclusions and original units. If the true independence unit is unresolved, do not claim a valid holdout.
