# Sensitivity to merger-event grouping and final-snapshot availability

This exploratory check joins two previously generated tables on their original event-row numbers: the [collision velocity-estimator comparison](../merger-collision-ensemble/README.md) and the [snapshot-99 fingerprint audit](../trajectory-grouping-audit/README.md). The input hashes are recorded in `summary.json`; the join checks row uniqueness and consistency of the recorded label. It uses h5py-derived tables from the same simulation, not independent datasets or observations. Reproduce with `python analyze.py` using NumPy and Matplotlib after generating both upstream tables.

Of 1,977 collision-comparison rows, 1,475 also have a valid exact final-snapshot fingerprint and 502 do not. The event-level median discrepancy is 407.4 km/s in the full collision sample, 351.8 km/s in the matched subset, and 572.0 km/s in the excluded subset. These subsets are selected by history and numerical validity, not randomized. This difference demonstrates a selection issue, not a causal effect of missingness or a correction factor.

On the same 1,475 rows, three distinct descriptive quantities are: the median over events, 351.8 km/s; the median of 317 recorded-label-specific medians, 349.3 km/s; and the median of 358 fingerprint-specific medians, 341.3 km/s. The latter two weight groups equally after first taking a within-group median; they are not weighted medians of all events and do not estimate the same quantity as the event-level median. None is a scientifically validated independent-cluster statistic: a label can contain multiple fingerprints, fingerprints do not prove a descendant or FoF identity, and repeated events are physically correlated. The thresholding and comparison are exploratory and not preregistered.

The discrepancy compares a potential-center position derivative with member-weighted subhalo velocity. It is neither a velocity measurement error nor evidence of dark-matter interactions. TNG-Cluster is a targeted zoom sample with documented coverage and contamination restrictions, not a uniform full-volume population ([official documentation](https://www.tng-project.org/data/cluster/)). Before a physical inference, resolve SubLink descendants, group membership, missing-history mechanism, tracer definitions and selection; reproduce a published [merger benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348194).
