## SIDM and gravitational lensing
**Area:** gravitational astrophysics, dark-sector particle phenomenology and structure formation.

A self-interacting dark matter (SIDM) particle can scatter off another dark-matter particle, potentially redistributing heat inside a gravitationally bound halo. In lensing, the measured quantity is distorted light, not a direct measurement of particle collisions. Distinguish halo mass, concentration, collapse phase, merger history and host-galaxy lensing uncertainty.

**Next validation:** independent halo-history splits plus a host-lens-constrained finite-source observable. [Question ledger](../hypotheses/README.md).

## Directional detectors and materials
**Area:** condensed-matter physics, quantum sensing and particle detection.

Light dark-sector candidates may scatter off **electrons** or excite **phonons**, quantized lattice vibrations, in solids. Crystal geometry restricts allowable responses but does not establish that any measurable scattering amplitude exists. Solar-day backgrounds can imitate directional modulation, so sidereal phase, exposure masks and controls matter.

**Next validation:** calibrated material response and time-dependent live-time before a sensitivity claim.

## Cosmological initial conditions
**Area:** inflationary cosmology, perturbation theory and large-scale structure.

A reduced small-scale galaxy population could reflect primordial power suppression or later changes in growth and survival. One late-time snapshot may not distinguish them.

**Next validation:** a specified early-versus-late pair of models and independent redshift/scale observables.

## Neutrino inference
**Area:** particle astrophysics and statistical inverse problems.

Catalog incompleteness, unconstrained backgrounds and wrong detector-response mappings can imitate new propagation effects. The corrected detector-response baseline must precede inference about exotic attenuation.

**Next validation:** reproduce a documented public IceTracks-DR2-v2 point-source benchmark with actual files before attempting new particle constraints.
