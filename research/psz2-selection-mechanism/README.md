# Why the PSZ2 bright-fraction response changes

The [bright-fraction sweep](../psz2-bright-fraction-sensitivity/README.md) found a small paired separation difference when T50 selects the brightest 1% of jointly eligible pixels, but a much larger difference at 5%. We decompose the southern selected pixels by whether they fall in the previously chosen 40-arcsecond circle around the radio-image difference peak. Both maps use the same selected sky pixels, but their own pixel values as center weights.

At **1%**, all 70 selected southern pixels lie within that circle. Both maps therefore estimate a center from the same confined region, giving a small pair difference of −1.34 kpc. At **5%**, 153 of 347 selected southern pixels fall in the circle. They account for **59.2% of the T50 position weight**, but **4.2% of the T50SUB position weight**. The remainder of the selected pixels is elsewhere in the aperture, allowing the two differently weighted centers to separate; the paired separation difference is −300.36 kpc. At 10%, the respective weight fractions are 39.8% and 2.0%. This is a concrete explanation of the estimator's non-monotonic response, not a physical identification of the source.

With T50SUB selecting pixels, none of the brightest 1–50% of jointly eligible southern pixels lie inside the 40-arcsecond circle; at 100% both reference images necessarily select the same full jointly eligible set. The exact results depend on the post-hoc circle, threshold, images, beam and ranking. Pixel weights are sums of correlated image pixel values, **not integrated fluxes**, and do not quantify uncertainty or statistical significance. This analysis does not establish the survey authors' selection, physical origin of the feature, cluster membership, a merger-age shift or a dark-matter constraint.

## Reproduce

Run `python research/psz2-selection-mechanism/analyze.py /path/to/psz2-maps /path/to/rrsamp.tsv` with NumPy, SciPy, Astropy and Matplotlib. The script validates image and catalog hashes, target beam and common support, and all 12 unmasked pair separations against the saved fraction sweep. It also checks the weighted-centroid identity for inside/outside groups and writes 48 rows of position-weight diagnostics and a plot. An independent Astropy calculation verified the 12 pair differences from those saved positions. Inputs are archived privately in Azure. Cite [Botteon et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11720), [Shimwell et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11733) and the [LoTSS release](https://lofar-surveys.org/planck_dr2.html).
