# Original survey regions and decision records

[Botteon et al., §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11720#S5.SS2) describe measuring relic integrated flux in polygonal regions enclosing the 2σ diffuse-emission contour, with attention to contamination and calibration artifacts. They define a relic position from the flux-weighted center of its brightest 10% of pixels. The paper text does not by itself provide the exact pixels, polygons, noise values or source exclusions for our two objects, and it should not be assumed that our circular, thresholded masks reproduce those choices. In particular, the paper's stated flux-integration region and its position definition are distinct statements; this inventory does not establish the exact region file used for either.

The official decision records linked from the [PSZ2 G181 page](https://lofar-surveys.org/clusters/PSZ2G181.06%2B48.47.html) and [MACS J1752 page](https://lofar-surveys.org/clusters/PSZ2G071.21%2B28.86.html) record **“n”** to the question asking whether there are significant calibration/subtraction artifacts in their classification workflows. The PSZ2 record contains an RR classification; the MACS file has RH and RR records. This is a survey classification judgment, not a pixel-level uncertainty model or proof that every local difference vanishes. Our processed-map comparisons do not contradict it or establish artifacts in either cluster.

We examined the SHA-256-verified **38,604,083-byte PSZ2 FITS archive already stored in Azure**, without extracting files. Its ten members are FITS images, including tapered and subtracted maps, a compact-model image and Chandra data. The member names include no explicit `.reg`, polygon, region or mask file. This establishes only the contents of this one archive, not that author regions or source files are unavailable elsewhere. The [MACS archive](../macs1752-processed-maps/manifest.json) was not re-inventoried in this check. A next source-level investigation should locate original polygon/mask definitions and RMS and imaging configuration for the same relics before claiming reproduction; author contact requires user approval for each email.

## Reproduce

Run `python research/survey-regions-and-decisions/inventory.py /path/official-fits.tar.gz /path/psz2-decision.txt /path/macs-decision.txt` with Python. It verifies the archive hash from the existing PSZ2 manifest, lists archive entries without extraction, checks the two saved linked decision records and writes `summary.json` with hashes and parsed fields. The two small decision records were retrieved from the official pages; their unchanged raw copies are saved privately in Azure. This is a release inventory and literature comparison, not a claim of complete survey-wide data availability, a correction to a published catalog, or an independent validation of classifications.
