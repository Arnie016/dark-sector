# MACS J1752: T50 and T50SUB image comparison

This exploratory paired test uses the [official cluster FITS package](https://lofar-surveys.org/clusters/PSZ2G071.21%2B28.86.html) and the published relic coordinates from [Botteon et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11720). The T50 and T50SUB filenames suggest different source-subtraction handling; the exact imaging and subtraction steps for these individual products have not been independently documented. Thus the comparison identifies differences between the two products, **not a measured effect attributable solely to compact-source subtraction**.

Both files are 1614×1614 Jy/beam images with 1.5-arcsecond pixels. Their coordinate transforms agree at three sampled image positions to within 0.00001 pixel; beam headers agree within numerical rounding (approximately 14.73 × 10.64 arcseconds). The same catalog-centered circles of 60, 90 and 120 arcseconds, finite-coverage masks, and 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 mJy/beam thresholds are applied to both images. Within each region the brightest 10% of eligible pixels are selected independently per image, and their brightness-weighted center is computed. The masks and thresholds are illustrative and do not replicate source-author masks or a noise model.

Across nine settings, paired center differences are **0.06–0.41 arcseconds north** and **0.01–15.90 arcseconds south**. In the 60-arcsecond southern circle they are 0.01–0.12 arcseconds; for 90- and 120-arcsecond circles they are 14.76–15.90 arcseconds. Signed pair-separation differences (T50 minus T50SUB) range from −15.80 to +0.20 arcseconds. These results show a region-dependent difference between these images for this estimator. They do not demonstrate a physical shift, source identity, catalog error, independent measurement, confidence interval, or merger-clock bias. Pixels are correlated; the maps share observations; the original masks and processing history remain necessary for causal interpretation.

Run `python research/macs1752-subtraction-sensitivity/analyze.py /path/to/selected-maps` with Astropy, NumPy and matplotlib. The directory must contain `T50kpc.fits` and `T50kpcSUB.fits`; code checks their SHA-256 digests. The 36 rows in `variants.csv` and top-10% counts were independently counted. The additional non-SUB 10,428,480-byte FITS was extracted by reading the one expected tar member and is stored privately in Azure with this analysis; the original archive and SUB file are already archived under [processed maps](../macs1752-processed-maps/README.md). The plot and analysis are versioned; no email was sent.
