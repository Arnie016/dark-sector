# PSZ2 G181.06+48.47: processed radio, X-ray and published lensing positions

The [LOFAR project cluster page](https://lofar-surveys.org/clusters/PSZ2G181.06%2B48.47.html) provides a 38,604,083-byte FITS tarball. It contains ten files, including the 50 kpc taper, discrete-source-subtracted radio map and a processed Chandra map. These are the survey team's products, not maps we derived from raw observations. [Botteon et al. (2022)](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2022/04/aa43020-21.pdf) describe source subtraction and image construction; [Jones et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07814) use 50 kpc taper images for relic analysis. The official project page currently displays 'No Chandra data available', despite the tarball containing a CHANDRA FITS file. That inconsistency is unresolved. The FITS header identifies Chandra ACIS, and its processing history references ObsID 22650; [Stroe et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.07651) also identify that observation for this target. Those facts support the association, but the processing and units have not been independently validated.

The radio image header reports JY/BEAM, a ~19.6 × 19.2 arcsecond beam, and a 143.75 MHz reference frequency. The Chandra FITS header lacks `BUNIT`; it is rendered with a relative intensity stretch, **not** calibrated physical X-ray units. Astropy reports inconsistent DATE-OBS and MJD-OBS metadata while interpreting that file's WCS. We retain the warning in `summary.json`. A coordinate transformation round-trip succeeds numerically, but is not independent evidence of absolute astrometric alignment. Do not use the X-ray image here for flux, photon counts, temperatures, shock detection, position uncertainties or offsets.

## Common-coordinate comparison

The plot marks two [Botteon/VizieR radio relic positions](../clock-crosspaper/README.md) with cyan crosses and the two [Ahn et al. (2025), Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.09067) weak-lensing **mass-peak coordinates** with green crosses. These are published positions plotted on two images, not newly measured centroids, a lensing convergence map, or independent measurements. The two displayed maps have different pixel scales, coverage, beams and display stretches; circles and colors do not encode uncertainties.

Using the rounded published coordinates, z = 0.234, and flat Ωm = 0.3, H0 = 70 km/s/Mpc gives a radio-position separation of 2.640 Mpc and a lensing-peak separation of 0.499 Mpc; their central ratio is 0.189. This is consistent in scale with the 0.50 Mpc and 2.70 Mpc separations tabulated by [Jee et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00093), whose measurement definitions and redshift handling may differ. The tabulations and maps share data and authors; this is not independent validation. We do not calculate a ratio error: lensing peak uncertainty, centroid covariance, redshift, astrometric uncertainty and radio source selection have not been propagated. Neither this ratio nor these maps constrain SIDM by themselves.

**Correction to the earlier study:** Ahn et al. directly report total-system R500c = 0.93 (+0.07, −0.08) Mpc in their Table 1. The independently calculated 0.931 Mpc agrees with the published rounded central value. The earlier note incorrectly said no directly quoted radius existed; it has been corrected.

## Reproduction and provenance

`manifest.json` pins the official tarball, selected radio and X-ray FITS files, and VizieR table by size and SHA-256. The complete official tarball, two selected maps, the VizieR table, and analysis were archived privately in Azure and verified by readback. GitHub contains the analysis and a derived annotated view, not the original FITS files. Run `python analyze.py radio.fits chandra.fits rrsamp.tsv` with Astropy, NumPy and matplotlib. Input hashes, image dimensions, coordinate bounds and WCS round trips are checked. A separate spherical geometry / cosmological distance calculation checked the numerical separations. `summary.json` records source header warnings. Check LOFAR and Chandra data attribution and reuse rules before publication; cite the survey, image-processing and lensing papers. No email was sent.
