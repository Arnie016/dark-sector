# Pericenter-distance unit and construction audit

The current [official TNG-Cluster documentation](https://www.tng-project.org/data/cluster/) labels `d_peri` in ckpc/h, derived from subhalo positions. The [Lee et al. paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.06340) describes fitting five points around closest approach. This audit finds a numerical pattern that merits verification against source code or the catalog authors; it does **not** establish the true unit, assert a documentation error, or authorize reinterpreting the field.

For each event with unique histories at snapshots `Snap_coll-1`, `Snap_coll`, and `Snap_coll+1`, finite positions in the 680,000 ckpc/h box and a finite positive `d_peri`, calculate nearest-image separation in the position field's documented comoving units and also multiply by `a/h` (h=0.6774) to obtain a proper-kpc numerical value. Compare the catalog number to each of the three samples under both representations. This is an exploratory numerical equality diagnostic, not a unit conversion. Catalog `d_peri` may be interpolated or computed using a different snapshot/algorithm; agreement is not independent observational proof.

Of 2,083 rows, 1,977 meet the criteria. Within an absolute numeric tolerance of 0.01, 1,428 have a nearby proper-scaled separation matching the catalog number, whereas zero have a nearby unscaled comoving separation matching. To reduce cases where the scale conversion is near unity, among the 1,442 selected rows with `|a(Snap_coll)/h - 1| > 0.1`, 1,023 match the proper-scaled numbers and zero match the unscaled ones. The filter is applied at the central snapshot while comparisons include adjacent snapshots; the tolerance is a diagnostic, not a physical measurement uncertainty. Event row 5 has catalog value 95.4061, raw central separation 93.0837, and proper-scaled central separation 95.4061. The match could reflect details of source computation, field conventions, or other effects; the origin is unresolved.

**Interpretation gate:** The previous [five-point pericenter prototype](../five-point-pericenter/README.md) directly compared its comoving fitted numerical distance with `d_peri` under the documentation label. Its reported distance differences are provisional and may compare differently scaled quantities; do not interpret them as errors or use them for a paper until the field's construction and units are independently established. Its window-to-window comparisons within the same representation remain separate method-sensitivity calculations.

Reproduce `python analyze.py /path/to/cluster_mergers.hdf5` with h5py/NumPy, using the adjacent snapshot redshift table. Catalog SHA-256 is checked and redshift SHA-256 recorded. Output is simulation-event data; repeated labels, pericenter selection, possible center-switch corrections, and availability cuts limit generalization. No dark-matter or observational result is inferred.
