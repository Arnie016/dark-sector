# MACS J1752: radio–optical position overlay

Using the archived, hash-checked T50 and T50SUB maps, we overlaid the LoTSS radio and associated optical positions, the nearby Keck galaxy and the previously selected T50 − T50SUB maximum. The Keck position is **3.92 arcseconds** from that maximum, the LoTSS optical position is **3.87 arcseconds**, and the LoTSS radio position is **5.28 arcseconds** away. All lie within one FWHM of the maps' **14.73 × 10.64 arcsecond** restoring beam. The LoTSS optical and Keck positions land on the same 1.5-arcsecond image pixel; they cannot be distinguished at this radio resolution.

The [overlay](radio-optical-overlay.png) shows that all three positions overlap the bright difference patch. The nearest-pixel differences are approximately 23.17 mJy/beam at the optical/Keck pixel and 19.53 mJy/beam at the radio position, compared with 26.55 mJy/beam at the selected maximum. These are pixel samples, not integrated fluxes or independent measurements; the display is clipped at 12 mJy/beam. The peak was selected post hoc, the pixel values are beam-correlated, and the two radio images share observations. The map therefore supports spatial consistency with the catalog association but cannot resolve whether the optical galaxy produces the radio emission or whether the subtraction patch changes a physical merger measurement. No chance-association probability or astrometric uncertainty is estimated.

## Reproduce

Run `python research/macs1752-radio-optical-overlay/analyze.py /path/to/selected-maps` with NumPy, Astropy and Matplotlib. The directory must contain the archived `T50kpc.fits` and `T50kpcSUB.fits`; the script validates their SHA-256 values, units, selected WCS points, dimensions and beam headers. Pixel axes are cutout coordinates; markers are placed via FITS celestial WCS. The 120-arcsecond field is centered on the post-hoc difference peak. Position and redshift provenance are in the [Keck cross-match](../macs1752-keck-crossmatch/README.md) and [LoTSS catalog check](../macs1752-photoz-v12/README.md). The survey maps and source documentation are linked in the [processed-map note](../macs1752-processed-maps/README.md); cite [Botteon et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11720) and the [LoTSS release](https://lofar-surveys.org/planck_dr2.html).

Coordinate-frame note: the map WCS returns FK5 J2000, while earlier peak-to-catalog comparisons treated stored numeric coordinates as ICRS. This can affect those positional offsets at about the 0.02-arcsecond level; see the [frame audit](../psz2-independent-reproduction/README.md).
