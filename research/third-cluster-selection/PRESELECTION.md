# Third-cluster candidate selection before image inspection

Selection recorded before reading the candidate images. From the six symmetric double-relic systems listed by [Botteon et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11720), exclude the two fields already examined (PSZ2 G071.21+28.86 and PSZ2 G181.06+48.47). The first candidate considered was PSZ2 G205.90+73.76; the authors explicitly discuss a 100 kpc-taper exception for both relics, and the official archive advertises 132,456,235 bytes. To retain a comparable 50 kpc product and respect the earlier 75,000,000-byte whole-file bound, move to the next cluster by ascending PSZ2 name: **PSZ2 G099.48+55.60**. Its official archive advertises 82,105,034 bytes, also above the whole-file cap. PSZ2 G113.91−37.01 and G165.46+66.15 advertise 262,872,683 and 115,014,314 bytes, respectively. These are HEAD metadata snapshots, not downloaded-content validation.

Try a bounded sequential stream of the G099 archive, stopping as soon as both exact T50kpc and T50kpcSUB FITS members are captured and never reading more than 75,000,000 compressed bytes; do not extract arbitrary archive paths. Abort if the members cannot be retrieved in that bound, the server fails, or source identity/units/coverage do not check out. The field was chosen after the first two results and after observing published descriptions; it is a targeted follow-up, not a preregistered, blinded, random, independent population or simulation holdout. If retrievable, use the previously documented 135 kpc beam, 300/450/600 kpc apertures, 0.1/0.2/0.3 mJy/beam thresholds and brightest-10% rule without tuning to this field's outcomes. Record any failure as a result, not a reason to silently choose a more convenient field.
