**DAMIC-M collaboration, K. Aggarwal et al., solar-reflected dark-matter study**: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09352v1 . Submitted 10 July 2026, accepted by Physical Review D on **2 October 2026**: https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/1h93-cx4x . Read Sec. The exposure is **1.257 kg-day collected October 2024 through January 2025**. Raw event files and detailed signal templates were NOT reproduced.

**DAMIC-M daily modulation, arXiv:2511.13962**: https://arxiv.org/html/2511.13962 , published August 19 2026. Different low-electron time statistic from the same observing campaign; not independent fresh exposure by publication date.

**Blanco et al., statistics of daily modulation, arXiv:2602.15947v1**: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15947v1 , submitted February 17 2026. Existing Poisson likelihood and correlated-background methods establish prior art. The current Poisson factorization is standard known probability theory.
