    How the measurement works

    Both maps are smoothed to a nominal 135 kpc beam and sampled on the T50SUB grid. The estimator finds pixels in a circular region around each published relic position, removes pixels below the threshold, then weights the selected brightest pixels by their flux. Pair separation comes from the angular distance between the north and south centers and the angular diameter distance.

    The two maps share observations. Circular regions, flux cuts and this choice of bright pixels are parameters of the experiment. The plotted differences measure processing sensitivity for these three fields. See the papers and methods ↗
