# Source and provenance registry

A claim is traceable only when its evidence path is traceable. For every primary source record: **canonical title; authors; DOI or arXiv version; published/updated date; date accessed; methods actually read; observations or simulations used; overlap with earlier datasets; public availability; code/data license; what the source does and does not establish**.

## Starting leads to verify, not automatic evidence

- SIDM Concerto: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10748
- Mergers Matter: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02566
- Directional material prior art: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15947
- IceCube data/software references: https://icecube.wisc.edu/ ; https://github.com/icecube
- JWST archive and calibrated observations: https://mast.stsci.edu/

These addresses are discovery pointers. Any future study must pin the exact version and methods it actually accessed rather than treating this seed list as a verified bibliography.

**Never** conflate paper publication with observation date or reuse of existing exposure with independent new evidence. Abstracts, press releases and old assistant citations do not substitute for likelihood files or measurement covariance.
